## How to fill this in

**Be specific.** "Green probably works" cannot be scored. "Muted sage and olive greens outperform bright greens" can.

**Guess where you don't know.** For the unfamiliar niche you will be guessing at nearly everything — that is expected and is exactly what makes it the strongest test. A wrong guess is a valid, useful data point.

**Do not research first.** Do not browse Etsy, do not look at competitors, do not check anything. Write what is in your head right now.

**Rate your confidence honestly** on each answer:
- `HIGH` — I would bet money on this
- `MEDIUM` — I lean this way but could be wrong
- `LOW` — this is close to a guess
- `NO IDEA` — genuinely no view *(a legitimate and useful answer)*
